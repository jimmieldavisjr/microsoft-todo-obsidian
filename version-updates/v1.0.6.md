# Version 1.0.6

Fixes the findings from Obsidian's community plugin review of [1.0.5](v1.0.5.md). There are no new features.

## Fixed

- **Task title box resizing** now uses Obsidian's `setCssStyles` instead of writing inline styles directly.
- **"Task added" notice** builds its *Open task* link with Obsidian's `createFragment` and `createEl` helpers.
- **Linked note summaries** treat a note's `description` or `summary` frontmatter as untyped until checked.
- **Styles**: completed tasks keep their strikethrough but drop the separate line colour, and tag labels drop a redundant fallback colour.

## Not changed

- The settings tab still uses `display()` and the disconnect button still uses `setWarning`. Their replacements (`getSettingDefinitions` and `setDestructive`) need Obsidian 1.13, and the plugin supports 1.7.2 and later.

## For developers

- The browser preview's Obsidian stand-in now provides `createFragment`, `setCssStyles` and fragment `createEl`, and no longer reads `navigator.platform`.

## Upgrading from 1.0.5

- Nothing to do. Your sign-in and settings carry over.
