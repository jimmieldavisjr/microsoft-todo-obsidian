# Version 1.0.4

A full redesign. Microsoft To Do now works like the real app inside Obsidian: your lists, your tasks and the selected task's details side by side. You can keep it in the main window, in the right side panel, or in both at once.

Compared with [1.0.3](v1.0.3.md).

## Highlights

- **Main window and side panel.** Open Microsoft To Do in a main tab, in the right side panel next to your other tools, or both at the same time. Both always show the same, up-to-date tasks.
- **Three-column layout.** Lists on the left, tasks in the middle, details on the right. In narrower spaces the lists become a drawer and details slide over the tasks.
- **Smart views.** My Tasks, Today, Important, Planned, Assigned to me and Flagged email, each spanning all your lists.
- **Richer tasks.** Subtasks, tags, notes and linked Obsidian notes, all edited in the details pane.
- **Search and filters.** Search every task in every list. Filter by All, Today, Upcoming, Overdue or Completed, and sort by due date, importance, title or date created.

## New

### Viewing
- **Main window and side panel at the same time.** New commands: *Open in main window* and *Open in side panel*. Each view also has a button to open the other.
- **Default location** setting, which chooses where the ribbon icon and the *Open* command take you.
- **Layout that adapts to its space**: three columns in a wide tab, two in a medium one, and one column with a list drawer in the side panel or on a phone.
- **Comfortable or compact rows**, switchable from the sort menu or in settings.

### Lists
- Create, rename and delete lists without leaving Obsidian.
- Give each list its own **colour and icon** (right-click a list).
- New smart views: **My Tasks** (everything open), **Planned** (anything with a due date), **Assigned to me** (Planner tasks, work and school accounts) and **Flagged email**.

### Tasks
- **Subtasks**: add, check off, rename and remove, with a progress bar.
- **Tags**, shown on each task as coloured labels.
- **Move a task** to another list.
- **Linked notes**: attach any Obsidian note to a task and open it from the task.
- **Add to note**: drops a link to the task into the note you were last editing.
- **Right-click a task** for quick actions: complete, mark important, set it due today or tomorrow, move, add to note, copy link, open in To Do, delete.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** inside the view: `Ctrl/Cmd+K` to search, `Ctrl/Cmd+Enter` for a new task, `Escape` to close.

### Finding things
- **Search** across all lists, including notes, tags and subtasks.
- **Filter tabs**: All, Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Completed.
- **Sorting** by due date, importance, title or date created, ascending or descending.
- **Sync status** at the foot of the list column, showing when tasks last synced. Click it to refresh.

### Commands
- New: *Open in main window*, *Open in side panel*, *Open My Tasks*.
- Renamed: *Open My Day* → *Open Today*. Existing hotkeys keep working.

## Changed

- **My Day is now called Today.** It always showed tasks due today or overdue rather than Microsoft's own My Day list, which other apps can't read. The new name says what it really shows.
- **Cleaner design.** A simple heading with the list's name, icon and open-task count replaces the old header. The interface follows your Obsidian theme and accent colour in light and dark modes.
- **Task details** open in their own pane instead of expanding inside the list.
- **Completed tasks** load the first time you open the *Completed* tab, which keeps refreshes fast.
- **Notes created from Obsidian** are now also recorded as a linked note on the task, as well as in the task's notes.

## Upgrading from 1.0.3

- **Nothing to set up again.** Your sign-in and settings carry over.
- **Where it opens**: the first time, Microsoft To Do opens in the main window. To use the side panel by default, change **Settings → Microsoft To Do → Default location** to *Right side panel*. Or open both with the two new commands.
- **Default view**: if it was set to *My Day*, it now opens *Today*, which shows the same tasks.
- **List colours and icons** are saved in Obsidian only. Microsoft To Do has nowhere to store them, so they won't appear in the Microsoft apps.
- **Moving a task gives it a new ID**, because Microsoft's service can't move tasks between lists. The plugin copies the task, with its subtasks, tags and links, then deletes the original. Links to the old task stop working.

## For developers

- **Browser preview** (`npm run preview`): a mock Obsidian window that runs the plugin's real components, styles and task logic against demo data, for checking UI changes without opening Obsidian. See `CLAUDE.md` for the rule that keeps it matching the plugin.
- **Two view types** share one task service: `microsoft-todo-view` (main window, same ID as before) and `microsoft-todo-sidebar` (side panel).
